Fishing techniques in Málaga

Six ways to fish the Mediterranean from the same boat, with a local guide who walks you through every step. Pick the one you like most or ask us which works best each season.

Bottom fishing

Sea bream, white bream and grouper close to the seabed. The easiest technique to learn, perfect if it's your first time or you're bringing kids.

See more

Live bait fishing

Live bait for red porgy, dentex and amberjack. It takes a little patience, but the reward is seriously big fish.

See more

Jigging

Metal jigs for horse mackerel, amberjack and dentex. A dynamic technique with movement and excitement on every drop.

See more

Squid fishing

Squid, cuttlefish and octopus on squid jigs. A relaxed, entertaining trip that families love.

See more

Trolling

Trolling lures as we cruise Málaga's coastline, chasing bonito, little tunny and bluefish.

See more

Tuna fishing

Bluefin tuna catch and release using heavy-duty tackle. The toughest challenge on offer, available in season only.
